# Legacy Codex — first-time demo guide (5 minutes)

## Before the cousin arrives (do this, not during)

1. Use the **owner account** on the demo device. Confirm Home does **not** show
   “This browser account cannot use AI yet.”
2. Open **Mission** and choose a real saved mission with a real finish line,
   evidence requirement and current saved action. Refresh once and confirm it
   survives.
3. Confirm the AI provider is configured (the model choice is visible; a failed
   request must not be presented as a successful demo).
4. If using live voice, grant microphone permission and test Start → Listening →
   End before the demo. Do not spend the demo debugging permission prompts.
5. Do not create demo missions, evidence, connections or lessons. If the real
   data is thin, demonstrate the records that exist and name what is missing.

## The five-minute path

**0:00 — One sentence and one screen.** Home is not a dashboard. It is the place
to ask about work that is already saved. Point at the current saved action, not
the whole menu.

**0:30 — Reconstruct the objective.** Open Mission and show the real mission:
the outcome, the finish line, the evidence requirement, and the starting note.
Say what Legacy Codex remembered without being retold.

**1:30 — Ask a real question.** Use Ask Codex or Triage on that mission. Keep
Economy unless the question needs depth. Point out the provider/tier label and
the **Why this?** reasoning. A prediction is not a commitment.

**3:00 — Save the next move.** Use the proposed action / Save as my next action.
Show the confirmation, then refresh and show that the same action and starting
note return. This is the difference between a chat answer and a durable
commitment.

**4:00 — Show inheritance only if it exists.** Open a real confirmed lesson,
correction or evidence-linked connection. Explain what was learned, where it
applies, and what it changes next time. If no such record exists, say “this
account has no confirmed lesson yet” and do not improvise one.

**4:45 — Close.** Summarize what was saved, what future interactions retrieve,
and what remains unverified. Stop at the real boundary.

## What not to do

- Do not demo on a fresh guest browser and call the sign-in gate a product
  failure; AI is owner-gated.
- Do not claim cross-project recognition or transferable learning without a real
  record.
- Do not show a cached/optimistic action as saved before the write returns.
- Do not use the 13-item menu as the demo path.

## Demo verdict

**NO-GO for an unattended external evaluator on a fresh account.** AI is
intentionally owner-only, and the owner mission/resume path was not verified in
this session.

**GO for an Eddie-driven, owner-signed-in demo** after the preflight above. If
any preflight item fails, show the saved records and say AI is unavailable; do
not substitute mock data.
